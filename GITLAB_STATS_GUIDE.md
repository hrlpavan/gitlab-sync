## 📊 Overview of the Contributor Banner

The **GitLab Contributor Platform** tracks and scores community contributions across 6 distinct categories to reward contributors, calculate leaderboard points, and award achievements.

| Metric | Current Score | What It Measures | Difficulty to Level Up |
| :--- | :---: | :--- | :--- |
| 💻 **Code** | `0` | Merge requests (MRs) authored and merged | Moderate |
| 📋 **Plan** | `4` | Issues opened, requirements planned, issue triage | ⭐ Easiest (Already Active) |
| 🤝 **Engage** | `0` | Community events, hackathons, blog posts, meetups | Easy |
| 💬 **Discuss** | `0` | Comments, MR reviews, forum replies, Discord | Easy |
| 🌍 **Translate** | `0` | UI string translations on Crowdin | Very Easy (Fast Points) |
| ⭐ **Other** | `0` | AI catalog components, CI templates, special awards | Varies |

## 1. 💻 Code (Currently: `0`)

### How it increments:
- Creating a **Merge Request (MR)** on any public project under [`gitlab-org`](https://gitlab.com/gitlab-org).
- Having an MR reviewed and merged by a GitLab team member.
- Committing code fixes, documentation fixes, or typo corrections.

### How to get your first points quickly:
1. **Find "Quick Win" Issues**:
   - Go to [GitLab Issues labeled `quick win`](https://gitlab.com/groups/gitlab-org/-/issues/?label_name%5B%5D=quick%20win) or [`good for new contributors`](https://gitlab.com/groups/gitlab-org/-/issues/?label_name%5B%5D=good%20for%20new%20contributors).
2. **Fix Docs & Typo Errors** (Simplest code contribution):
   - Navigate the [GitLab Docs Project](https://gitlab.com/gitlab-org/gitlab-docs).
   - If you spot a broken link, typo, or outdated screenshot, click **Edit** and submit a Merge Request.
3. **Contribute to Tools & Plugins**:
   - Small fixes to [`gitlab-vscode-extension`](https://gitlab.com/gitlab-org/gitlab-vscode-extension) or [`gitlab-runner`](https://gitlab.com/gitlab-org/gitlab-runner).

## 2. 📋 Plan (Currently: `4`)

### Why you already have 4 points:
You received points here for creating issues, filing bug reports, or structuring project milestones in GitLab.

### How to increase it further:
- **Report Bugs**: If you encounter an unexpected issue in GitLab, open a detailed bug report on [GitLab Issues](https://gitlab.com/gitlab-org/gitlab/-/issues).
- **Propose Features**: Submit actionable feature proposals with detailed use-cases.
- **Triage Community Issues**: Help label issues or verify whether older bugs can still be reproduced on recent GitLab versions.

## 3. 💬 Discuss (Currently: `0`)

### How it increments:
- Adding comments to public issues or Merge Requests in the `gitlab-org` group.
- Participating in reviews on open community Merge Requests.
- Answering questions on the **GitLab Community Forum** ([forum.gitlab.com](https://forum.gitlab.com)).
- Helping community members in the official **GitLab Discord Server** ([discord.gg/gitlab](https://discord.gg/gitlab)).

### How to get your first points quickly:
1. Join the [GitLab Community Forum](https://forum.gitlab.com) using your GitLab account.
2. Reply to 1–2 user questions in the *How-to & Troubleshooting* category.
3. Join [discord.gg/gitlab](https://discord.gg/gitlab), connect your GitLab handle in the verification channel, and help answer questions in `#contributing` or `#general`.

---

## 4. 🌍 Translate (Currently: `0`) — *The Fastest Zero-to-One*

### How it increments:
- Translating GitLab UI strings into other languages (e.g. Hindi, Kannada, Spanish, German, French, etc.) via **Crowdin**.
- Approving or proofreading translated strings.

### How to get your first points in 5 minutes:
1. Visit the [GitLab Crowdin Project](https://crowdin.com/project/gitlab-ee).
2. Sign in using your **GitLab account (`hrlpavan`)**.
3. Pick any language you are fluent in.
4. Translate 5 to 10 untranslated strings and save them.
5. Crowdin automatically syncs with the GitLab Contributor Platform to increment your **Translate** count.

## 5. 🤝 Engage (Currently: `0`)

### How it increments:
- Registering and participating in **GitLab Hackathons** (held periodically with prize tiers and swag).
- Attending virtual **GitLab Community Office Hours** or meetups.
- Publishing technical content (e.g., writing a Dev.to, Medium, or Hashnode tutorial about GitLab CI/CD, Antigravity + GitLab, or DevSecOps).
- Speaking at a meetup or hosting a community webinar.

### How to get your first points:
1. Look out for the next [GitLab Hackathon](https://about.gitlab.com/community/hackathon/).
2. Publish a short article or LinkedIn post showing how you set up `git dual-sync` between GitLab & GitHub or how you use Antigravity with GitLab MCP.
3. Submit your content link to the GitLab Developer Relations / Community team on Discord.
